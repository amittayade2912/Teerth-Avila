About the project

A residential address in Sus, Pune

Teerth Realties describes Teerth Avila as a residential development between Sus Hills and Baner Link Road. Public records place the project at Sus, Mulshi, Pune, near Vidya Valley School Road.

The developer identifies the project with MahaRERA registration P52100034662. Public RERA-related records identify a project area of 22,926 sq. m and four building records.
